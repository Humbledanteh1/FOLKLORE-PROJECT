# Folklore Landing Page — Design Direction

## Three initial approaches

### Theme Name: Botanical Field Guide
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm editorial marketplace that treats every agent like a documented specimen: practical, human, and earned through evidence. Parchment, forest ink, and hand-drawn marks give the interface the intimacy of a trusted archive.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Quiet Operations Room
**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, high-contrast operations console with paper-white surfaces, disciplined data blocks, and a precise green signal color. The emotional intent is confidence without hype.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Signal in the Canopy
**Very Brief Intro:** A darker, image-led direction where the supplied aerial landscape becomes a living map and vivid chartreuse accents mark trusted routes through e-commerce work. It feels cinematic and exploratory without becoming cyberpunk.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Selected approach: Botanical Field Guide

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design with field-guide ephemera, quiet Swiss discipline, and a tactile natural-history archive sensibility.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence over spectacle:** Every claim is paired with a record, task, or clear operational outcome.
2. **Tactile clarity:** Parchment textures, fine rules, catalog numerals, and ink-like marks make the digital interface feel authored.
3. **Asymmetric reading rhythm:** Split compositions, offset blocks, and image-led wayfinding replace generic centered SaaS layouts.
4. **Motion with restraint:** Motion should feel like paper sliding into place or a field note being revealed; never decorative for its own sake.

### Color Philosophy
The palette turns the supplied image into a working system: deep forest ink gives authority, oxidized teal carries trust, pollen gold signals attention, parchment makes the page human, and rust provides a rare editorial punctuation mark. The signature green is not a generic tech accent; it is the color of the canopy seen from above.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a full-bleed aerial hero with a floating, offset editorial rail; follow it with staggered category cards, a dark archive band, and a split “how it works” section. Let content align to an invisible left-hand spine while image and proof blocks deliberately break the grid.

### Signature Elements
1. A bird-and-leaf field mark used as the brand icon and as a small section seal.
2. Catalog numerals, thin rules, and “told, tested, trusted” archive labels.
3. Quiet image crops with soft grain and paper-edge transitions rather than hard card-heavy compositions.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should reward curiosity: tabs change the featured agent record without jarring movement, buttons compress subtly on press, and expandable proof details reveal more context without sending the user away. Every interactive element remains keyboard reachable and provides visible focus.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for hover, press, and tab changes. On first load, fade and lift hero copy in a short stagger; allow the image to drift only 1–2% on scroll for depth. Keep decorative marks nearly still. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling non-essential transforms and entrance movement.

### Typography System
Use **Fraunces** for headlines, agent names, and editorial statements; use **Space Mono** for labels, numerals, metadata, and CTAs; use **DM Sans** for readable body copy. Headlines should be compact and expressive, body copy should stay between 15–18px, and mono labels should use generous tracking and uppercase sparingly.

### Brand Essence
**Folklore is a field guide to dependable AI agents for e-commerce operators who want proof before promises; it turns operational tools into stories you can inspect.**

Personality: **observant, grounded, quietly bold**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound declarative and human, CTAs sound like invitations into a record, and microcopy names what is actually happening. Avoid generic growth language and unsupported superlatives.

Example lines:

> “Every agent here has a story.”

> “Open the record before you hand over the work.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark uses a high-contrast editorial serif with a slightly italicized terminal on “lore”; the symbol is a compact bird crossing a circular archive seal, paired with a single leaf gesture. The mark should remain legible at favicon size without relying on the wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color
**Canopy Green — #2F6F62.** A blue-leaning forest green sampled from the emotional center of the supplied aerial image, used for trust, active states, and verified signals.
